Management System for harnessing the benefits of AI in organizations while managing the risks

## TLDR

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is one of the most powerful technology of the 21st century. AI companies have been on the path of ever accelerating improvements in large language model (LLM) performance. Chip manufacturers, hyper-scalers, energy providers and other parts of the eco-system have been scrambling to keep up. 
Few have raised concerns on the risks of such fast-paced development of intelligence with risks of annihilation as one of the extreme outcomes being promoted by some. 
Heavy handed approach of centralized regulation by government or international bodies is bound to affect the development. 
The diffusion of the benefical aspects of this technology. 
A more practical, reasonable and straightforward solution is to adapt frameworks of management systems that has been well defined, accepted, established and demonstrated efficacy in several industries such as aerospace, healthcare, food processing. 
This document outlines a management system for enterprises, any organization deploying AI.
 Management systems provide a systematic approach to harness the power of AI while managing the risks.   

## Scope
The proposal confines itself to organizations considering adoption of AI solutions to achieve well-defined outcomes. 
Enterprises, corporations, non-profits, medium and small businesses. 
This is to ensure the discussions are bounded and aligned to specific business or other organization outcomes.

Research and Development on intelligence, super-intelligence, academic or otherwise, may choose to adopt this or a derivative system.Moral, ethical, philosophical aspects are not discussed.

## Playing with Fire
Earliest tribes that discovered fire would have felt the negative consequences of this technology. The leaf skirt burning down. 
Or the tree where the tribe lived. Perhaps entire forest. Who knows ? There are no written records documenting these early mishaps. 
Rules were discussed. Do's and don't with cultural norms established within communities that saved complete annihilation of the human race. The benefits, on the other hand, were immense. The modern framework for fire safety has distilled the knowledge into the [Fire Triangle][fire-triangle]. Three factors to be managed to prevent fires - Oxygen, Fuel, Heat. 
That is the arc of every technology discovered or invented by humans. 

## The Bhasmasura Effect 
Software eats the world should have raised the alarms more broadly and much earlier. 

The regulated industries such as healthcare, aerospace did see the risks of increased failures due to software and 
updated the relevant standards, introduced new ones to manage those risks. 

Social media with its viral and network effects has created cultural and societal risks that are being felt 
decades after their mass diffusion. Corrective measures are now being taken by some nations. 

Software development became the first major use case for AIs which has created the most recent alarm bells being sounded 
where the pace and potential scale of  these software systems impact has raised questions on number of aspects - jobs, environmental sustainability, control, identity, organization structures. 

Like [Bhasmasura][basura] who turns everyone to ash by touching their head with the power given to him and is eventually destroyed by 
gods who trick him to place his hand on his own head, AI seems to be hurtling down this path. 

## Management Systems

Healthcare, Aerospace , food processing industries have evolved to have industry specific management system standards.
Often called Quality Management Systems, to address safety and performance requirements of products and services.
ISO 13485, AS 9100, cGMP, HACCP, are few examples of globally accepted management systems adopted by industries in specific domains.

Management systems offer an elegant solution to address the need for control while implementing systemic changes. 
While they add friction to the pace of development, sweeping changes do require thoughtful progress to make meaningful and intended impact. 
As the management system is adopted within the confines of an organization with periodic external independent inspection 
of its implementation efficacy, a management system for intelligence provides the perfect antidote to the issues being foreseen with AI.

## Intelligence Management System

The proposal is to design, review and adopt an Intelligence Management System horizontally across all enterprises. This systems based
approach provides a professional pathway to deploy and leverage intelligence solutions for achieving organization outcomes.
Key elements of the Intelligence Management System are described in the next sections.

### Six Systems

Inspired by the Pharmaceutical cGMP six-systems inspection model, the following six systems are proposed

| **No.** | **System** | **Description** |
| :-:   | :--       | :------------      |
| 1.      | Intelligence Management System | Policy, Objectives, plans and procedures establishing the organization's intelligence activities|
| 2.      | Infrastructure System | Power, data center, servers, chips, networking, security, cloud management software - foundation elements required to run and manage intelligence solutions.|
| 3.      | Supply Control System | Managing third-party and/or outsourced suppliers of products and services such as LLMs, MCPs/APIs, agents, harnesses, evals, cloud services, power, chips.|
| 4.      | Intelligence Development System | Plans and procedures for design, development and lifecycle activities of intelligence solutions with in-house, forward deployed or outsourced engineering teams.|
| 5.      | Evaluation System | Development, management, execution, monitoring and reporting of evals specific to the organization's intelligence solutions which include LLM-as-judge systems, human reviewers.|
| 6.      | Deployment System | Developing, managing and monitoring development sandboxes, other dev-ops/ML-ops/AI-ops kind of activities.|

### Five Layers

[AI is a five layer cake][five-layer-cake-blog] as postulated by Jensen Huang. Quoting from the blog post

> AI runs on real hardware, real energy and real economics. It takes raw materials and converts them into intelligence at scale. Every company will use it. Every country will build it. 


The five layers are 

1. Energy
2. Chips
3. Infrastructure
4. Models
5. Apps

The six systems cover the 5 layers to provide a complete coverage of the intelligence layers.

### Four Factors

The Intelligence Management System effectiveness is determined by four factors

| **No.** | **Factor** | **Description** |
| :-:   | :--       | :------------      |
| 1.      | Leadership | Sets the strategy, defines objectives, allocates resources, ensures alignment, reviews effectiveness of the intelligence management system.|
| 2.      | Resources | Adequate resources including human, compute, capital need to be allocated and available. Necessary training and development of personnel, maintenance of infrastructure also to be planned.| 
| 3.      | Intelligence Operations | Design, develop, test, deploy intelligence solutions aligned to organization objectives.|
| 4.      | Evaluation Activities | A continuous feedback loop with robust eval systems enable continuous improvement. Monitoring, tracking alignment, transparent reporting enable state of control. |

### Three Profiles

The depth and rigour of management system can be tuned based on the organization's objectives.

Three profiles are proposed that should cover majority of the organizations. Each organization can select the suitable profile and communicate within its organization. The profile selection drives the set of activities across the organization.

1. **Experience Critical** for consumer focused enterprises, non-critical services 
2. **Mission Critical** for essential services such as financial services, security, energy
3. **Life Critical** for healthcare, defense,aerospace 

Existing standards and its conscious implementation may already provide sufficient controls for Life Critical profile adopting organizations. 
Specific clauses, tools and practices may help further improve their processes.

The other two profiles would benefit in implementing appropriate variations of this system.

### Two Imperatives

1. Alignment : across the organization as well as in the specifics of each activity. Example the business objective tracing down to a system prompt, reward function provides necessary alignment within the organization.

2. The human harness : fancy word for human in the loop , teamwork. Human review and approval is necessary to ensure alignment, 
effectiveness of evals and several aspects of the management system to be implemented effectively.

### One Objective 

Primary objective is to achieve a state of control across the organization's activities. 
A systematic,systems based approach makes it feasible to achieve a state of control with intelligence systems . 

## Call to action

1. Critique and improve the management system.
2. Build consensus among stakeholders and move towards adoption.
3. Establish formal management systems through appropriate means.
